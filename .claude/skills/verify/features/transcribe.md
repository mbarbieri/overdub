# Transcribe mode

Transcribe mode is the third mode, beside Record and Edit, for learning a part by ear from the Song's Backing Tracks (ADR 0010). Its page shows an overview of the whole Song on top, with the heard mix's waveform and a rectangle for the visible window, then a ruler over one tall, zoomable waveform of the heard mix, and a right column with Practice Speed and Transpose that the user can hide. Transcribe mode never changes a Clip: split, delete, nudge and the other Clip edits are refused there.

## Sub-features

- `transcribe-mode`: `3` or the top bar's **Transcribe** button runs `show_mode mode=transcribe`. `/view/mode` is `transcribe`, and `ui.mode_changed` names it `transcribe`.
- `transcribe-overview`: clicking or dragging the overview moves the visible window there (`set_transcribe_view`). The window is `/view/transcribe` (`first_sample`, `samples_per_pixel`).
- `transcribe-zoom`: the wheel scrolls time, and ⌘-wheel or a trackpad pinch zooms around the pointer (`set_transcribe_view`), in until about 2 s fills the width and out until the whole Song fits. The socket's values are clamped the same way.
- `transcribe-seek`: clicking the ruler or the waveform seeks (`seek`). Space plays and stops, as in the other modes.
- `transcribe-column`: the right column's Practice Speed and Transpose run `set_practice_speed` and `set_transpose`, as the top bar's fields do. `I` runs `set_transcribe_column_visible` with the opposite of `/view/transcribe_column_visible`. The choice is remembered per Mac in `window-settings.json` beside the Last Song (`OVERDUB_WINDOW_SETTINGS_FILE`; a scripted launch without it neither reads nor writes it), never in the Song.
- `transcribe-focus-mix` (issue #226): the right column lists every Track in Song order with ◎ (Focus), M and S. ◎ runs `set_focus_track track_id=<id>` (◎ on the Focus Track again, or `track_id` null or absent, clears it), M `set_transcribe_mute`, S `set_transcribe_solo`. Picking a Focus Track solos it alone in the Transcribe Mix; mutes stay. The Focus Track is `/song/song/focusTrackId`, the mix each Track's `transcribeMute` and `transcribeSolo`; none is an undo step, and `song.json` keeps them. While the mode is `transcribe`, `/transport/transcribe_mix_heard` is true and the engine plays the Transcribe Mix's mute and solo instead of the Song's; Record, Edit and Export play the Song's own mix. The waveform shows the Focus Track under its name, or "Transcribe Mix" when there is none.
- `octave-shift` (issue #233): under Transpose the right column shows −1, 0, +1 and +2 for the Focus Track (disabled without one); each runs `set_octave_shift track_id=<Focus Track> octaves=<n>` (−1..+2, else `song.octave_shift_out_of_range`). Each Track keeps its own (`/song/song/tracks/N/octaveShift`, format 12, outside history, saved), so moving the Focus and back brings back each Track's. Only the Focus Track's shift is heard, only in Transcribe mode: `/transport/octave_shifted_track` names it. It is rendered offline in one pass with the heard Transpose and Practice Speed into a temp file outside the package (`song.practice_speed_started` with `octave_shift`); until then that Track plays silent, `/transport/octave_rendering` is true and the column shows a spinner. Record, Edit and Export never hear it.
- `band-filter` (issue #234): under the octave shift the right column shows FILTER: an On toggle and the low and high cut fields in Hz for the Focus Track (disabled without one). Each runs `set_band_filter track_id=<Focus Track> on= low_hz= high_hz=` with the other values kept; both cuts are 20..20000 Hz (else `song.band_filter_out_of_range`) and the low below the high (else `song.band_filter_low_not_below_high`). Each Track keeps its own (`/song/song/tracks/N/bandFilter` `{on, lowHz, highHz}`, default off at 100..4000 Hz, format 13, outside history, saved). Only the Focus Track's is heard, only in Transcribe mode and while on: `/transport/heard_band_filter` is `{track_id, low_hz, high_hz}` then, else null. It runs live in the engine (a high-pass and a low-pass biquad on that Track's strip, no render, no plan rebuild). Record, Edit and Export never hear it.
- `markers` (issue #227): a Loop/Marker strip sits above the ruler in every mode (Record: above the overview, read-only). `K` runs `add_marker` ("Marker N" at the playhead, playback keeps going, `ui.marker_added`; replies `created_id`). On the strip in Transcribe and Edit, double-click a Marker to rename it (`rename_marker`), drag it to move it (`move_marker`), and click it then backspace to delete it (`delete_marker`). Clicking a Marker runs `select_marker`, which seeks there (in Record that moves the record point) and selects it outside Record (`/view/selected_marker`). Transcribe's left column lists the Markers in time order; clicking a row runs `select_marker`. Markers are `/song/song/markers` (format 8), each change one undo step (`Add Marker`, `Move Marker`, `Rename Marker`, `Delete Marker`). Record mode refuses the four edits with `ui.record_mode`, shown as a Notice.
- `loops` (issue #228): the strip shows each Loop as a bar over its range in every mode (Record read-only). In Transcribe and Edit, drag across an empty part of the strip to run `add_loop start= end=` ("Loop N", replies `created_id`, `ui.loop_added`), or press `[` (`mark_loop_start`, `/view/loop_start_pending`) and `]` (`mark_loop_end`) while listening; Esc runs `cancel_loop_start`. Making a Loop activates it. Drag a bar's edge to resize it (`resize_loop`), double-click it to rename it (`rename_loop`), backspace deletes the active Loop in Transcribe or the selected one in Edit (`delete_loop`); each is one undo step (`Add Loop`, `Resize Loop`, `Rename Loop`, `Delete Loop`). Clicking a bar or a Loop card (Transcribe's left column, above the Markers), option-left / option-right and option-1..9 run `select_loop` / `select_adjacent_loop`: in Transcribe that activates the Loop (`/song/song/activeLoopId`, `looping` true, waveform zoomed to fit, playhead to its start unless inside); in Edit it selects it (`/view/selected_loop`); in Record it moves the record point. `L` runs `set_looping`. While the mode is `transcribe` and looping, `/transport/heard_loop` is `{start, end}` and playback wraps there; Space starts at the Loop's start unless the playhead is inside it. Record mode refuses making and editing Loops with `ui.record_mode`.
- `loop-speed` (issue #230): each Loop keeps its Practice Speed (`/song/song/loops/N/practiceSpeedPercent`, format 10, outside history). A new Loop takes the current Practice Speed; activating a Loop in Transcribe mode (or `L` / entering Transcribe while it loops) sets the app state's `practice_speed` to it; changing the Practice Speed (field, `H`, `-`/`=`) while it loops changes the Loop's too (`ui.loop_practice_speed_set`). Leaving Transcribe for Record or Edit sets 100%. Each Loop card shows its speed.
- `transcribe-refusals`: in Transcribe mode `split_selected_clip`, `delete_selection`, `nudge`, `move_clip`, the trims, fades, gain and `take_lane_click` answer `ui.transcribing` (field `op`, message "Transcribe mode never changes a Clip") and change nothing.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Press `3`, or click **Transcribe** in the top bar, after Record and Edit.
- Press `I` to show or hide the right column.

## Driving it with overdub-ctl

Preconditions:

- `launch.sh` and `doctor.sh` passed. Make a Song with `new_song_at`, set the Master volume to its minimum, and `import_files` a synthetic WAV.

1. `show_mode mode=transcribe`, then read `state`: `/view/mode` is `transcribe`.
2. `set_transcribe_view first_sample=0 samples_per_pixel=1`: `/view/transcribe/samples_per_pixel` is the finest zoom (about 2 s across the waveform), never 1.
3. `seek sample=48000`: `/transport/position` is 48000.
4. `set_transcribe_column_visible visible=false`: `/view/transcribe_column_visible` is false.
5. `split_selected_clip` answers `ui.transcribing`, and `song.json` is unchanged.
6. `set_transcribe_mute track_id=<the imported Track> mute=true`: `/transport/transcribe_mix_heard` is true, the Master meter falls to silence while playing, and the Track's own `mute` stays false. `show_mode mode=edit` and it is heard again.
7. `set_focus_track track_id=<id>`: `/song/song/focusTrackId` is that id and only that Track has `transcribeSolo` true.

## Gotchas

- No journey script drives Transcribe mode yet: every sub-feature is **unverified** in the real app. Tests cover them (`tests/ui/TranscribeModeTest.cpp`, `tests/ui/TranscribeMixTest.cpp`, `tests/ui/OctaveShiftTest.cpp`, `tests/ui/BandFilterTest.cpp`, `tests/engine/EngineBandFilterTest.cpp`, `tests/ui/MarkersTest.cpp`, `tests/ui/LoopsTest.cpp`, `tests/ui/LoopPracticeSpeedTest.cpp`, `tests/engine/EngineLoopTest.cpp`, `tests/ui/TranscribeGeometryTest.cpp`, `tests/control/IntentOpsTest.cpp`, the gesture census).
- The waveforms are drawn from the peaks cache with the Transcribe Mix's mute and solo (or the Focus Track alone), the Song's volumes and Clip gains, not from the audio heard: fades and pan are not drawn.
- The view does not follow the playhead during playback.
